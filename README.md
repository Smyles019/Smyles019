# Hi there, I'm Allger Symles Migide 👋

I'm a final-year BSc Informatics and Computer Science student at Strathmore University, building at the intersection of AI systems and real-world financial infrastructure. I lead the AI layer on NORT, a multi-agent advisory platform bridging prediction markets with African payment rails, and I care most about closing the gap between cutting-edge AI and everyday use — especially in African contexts.

- 🔭 **Current Focus:** Leading the AI agent layer on NORT (multi-agent orchestration across FastAPI + Next.js), and wrapping up my capstone on XGBoost-driven developmental risk prediction for children under five.
- 🌱 **Learning & Exploration:** Rust, distributed systems fundamentals (Raft, Paxos), and deeper work with the x402 payment protocol.
- ⚡ **Core Interests:** AI/ML engineering, fintech & payments infrastructure, distributed systems, and cloud infrastructure.
